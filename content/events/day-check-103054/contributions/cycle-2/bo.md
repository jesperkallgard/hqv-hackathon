One column, four cards, nothing else. An outsider lands here and knows where to click: What's changed, What we're testing, How we do things, Who owns what.

- The front door is four cards in one column — no hero, no search box, no welcome text, nothing above or beside them.
- The four are named exactly: What's changed, What we're testing, How we do things, Who owns what. Nothing added, nothing renamed.
- Each card shows only its name and the date it was last updated. No description, no icon, no preview line, no count.
- What's changed doesn't narrow. Testing and ownership cards open the same mixed feed, filtered — not a second store of content.
- How we do things is the one genuinely new area — it holds process, not dated events, so it doesn't live in the feed at all.

## What it is
The home page. Four flat panels stacked in one column, top to bottom, each one a door into an area. Nothing else on the page.

## Card content
Each card: the area's name, and the date it was last updated. That's the whole card.

- What's changed — updated 12 March
- What we're testing — updated 12 March
- How we do things — updated 1 March
- Who owns what — updated 1 March

## The assumption we're making, unresolved by the room
We didn't get a clear answer on whether "What's changed" narrows to just site changes now that there are separate cards for testing and ownership. We're going with: it doesn't narrow. The canon already decided the "What's changed" feed mixes changes, tests, and ownership on purpose, in one list, with no sections. We're keeping that. So "What we're testing" and "Who owns what" are doors into that same feed, filtered to show only the rows of that kind — not new, separate content that would duplicate what's already there. If the room wants "What's changed" to mean only site changes, that's a real change to what got built in sprint 1 and should be said out loud, not assumed again next sprint.

"How we do things" is different in kind: it's process — how something is normally handled — not an event with a date something happened. It doesn't belong in the feed and isn't built from it.

## Look and feel
Follows the direction: white, sparse, a lot of air between the four cards. Navy heading on each card, left-aligned. Flat panel, no shadow, no border-radius flourish. One column only — not a grid, not side-by-side, even on a wide screen. It should look like a list of four names someone wrote down, not four tiles someone designed.

## Out of scope
- No icons on the cards.
- No descriptions or preview text under the name.
- No counts ("12 items") or badges.
- No search across the four areas.
- No fifth card, no "more" link, no settings.
