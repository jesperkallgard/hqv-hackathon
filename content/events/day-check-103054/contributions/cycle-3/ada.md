Every post is seven things, but the feed only ever shows three. The other four wait until you open the post, on its own page.

- A post is seven things, not one line: headline, audience, a three-line summary, a status or date, an owner by name, how often it's updated, and one link onward.
- The feed shows three of the seven — headline, owner, date — so scanning stays a list, not a wall of text.
- The other four — audience, summary, status, link onward — live on the post's own page, opened by clicking the headline.
- The link onward points outside this site, to the real doc, ticket or tool it's about — never back into another page of this feed.
- Audience answers "who is this for" in plain words. It's read, not a tag you filter by.

## The seven fields
1. **Headline** \u2014 short, plain, what changed. e.g. "My Husqvarna login moved".
2. **Audience** \u2014 who needs to know. e.g. "Anyone who signs into My Husqvarna."
3. **Three-line summary** \u2014 what changed and what it meant, not what was done.
4. **Status or date** \u2014 when it happened, or where it stands right now.
5. **Owner** \u2014 one person, by name.
6. **How often it's updated** \u2014 the cadence this line gets revisited, separate from the date above.
7. **One link onward** \u2014 the actual doc, ticket, or tool this post is about.

## List view vs. post page
The feed (built in sprint 1) shows only headline, owner and date on each row \u2014 that page does not change. Clicking a headline takes you to that post's own page, where the other four fields appear: audience, the three-line summary, status, and the link onward. This is a page you go to, in keeping with "a page you go to, not one that comes to you" \u2014 not a row that expands in place. That's our call on the open question from sprint 1; nobody in the room has decided it either way yet, so flag it if another group reads it differently.

## Example post (real content, not a placeholder)
- **Headline:** My Husqvarna login moved
- **Audience:** Anyone who signs into My Husqvarna
- **Three-line summary:** Login moved from the old portal to the new one on 1 March. Old bookmarks stop working at the end of March. Password reset emails now come from the new domain, not the old one.
- **Status or date:** Rolled out 1 March
- **Owner:** Jonas B
- **How often it's updated:** Monthly
- **Link onward:** IT help pages \u2014 Login & access

In the feed, this row shows: "My Husqvarna login moved" \u2014 Jonas B \u2014 1 March. Opening it shows all seven.

## Out of scope
- No comments, reactions, or version history on a post page.
- No separate "audience" filter across the feed \u2014 audience is written on the post, not used to narrow the list.
- No rule yet for what "status" looks like when a post has no fixed date (e.g. an ongoing test) \u2014 open, same as the date-format question sprint 1 left open.

## Look
Post page is one column, white, sparse, navy heading for the headline. Fields run down the page in the order above, plain text, no boxes or shadows, no icons next to the field labels \u2014 it should read like a memo, not a form.
