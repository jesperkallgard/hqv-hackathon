The first screen is a four-card front door in one column. It replaces the mixed feed with separate entry points so somebody knows where to click straight away.

- The front door shows four areas and no more: What's changed, What we're testing, How we do things, and Who owns what.
- Each area is a card in a single column, and each card shows the last update date.
- An outsider can read the four names and know where to click without asking a colleague.
- The page is built for speed: somebody should get the answer on one screen without scrolling.
- It stays unclear whether splitting What's changed from What we're testing also changes the feed pages themselves, or only the front door.

Use the names exactly as given: What's changed, What we're testing, How we do things, Who owns what. Make the first screen only, with four cards in one generous column. Keep the answer on one screen with no scrolling. Show the last update date on each card. Do not add any other areas. The only open point is whether the split carries over into the feed pages themselves; that is not decided here.
