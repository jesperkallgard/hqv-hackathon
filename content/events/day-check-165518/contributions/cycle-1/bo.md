One internal site, called What's Changed. It answers "what changed, who owns it, how often" so nobody has to ask a colleague.

- One site with a header naming it What's Changed and three ways in: the feed, who owns what, how we work.
- The homepage is the feed itself — what changed this week, what's being tested, who owns what — newest first, every row carrying an owner and a date.
- It refuses to be a dashboard. No charts, no metrics tiles — just the feed.
- The one orange pill is Post an update — the primary action is adding a change, not reading one.
- A footer names who keeps the site running, so the site itself has an owner.

## Structure
- **Header**: site name "What's Changed", left-aligned, with a short nav: Feed, Who owns what, How we work.
- **Feed** (the homepage): a single generous column of rows, newest first. Each row: what changed, an owner name, a date, and a small round state tag (e.g. "Live", "Testing", "Planned") — never a coloured word.
- **Who owns what**: a plain list mapping each part of the product to the person who owns it and how often it's updated. Example row: "Dealer locator — owned by [name] — updated weekly."
- **How we work**: short, plain notes on process — not a wiki, not marketing copy.
- **Footer**: names who keeps the site itself running.

## The one primary action
The single orange pill button is **Post an update**. Posting a change is the thing this site wants you to do, not just read — everything else (Who owns what, How we work) is outlined navy, secondary.
*Assumption: the group didn't specify this explicitly when asked — this is the sensible fit given the feed is the whole site. Open to being corrected next round.*

## Content, in their words
- Example feed rows: "What changed this week", "What's being tested", "Who owns the dealer locator".
- Rows read like sentences a colleague would say out loud, not ticket titles.

## Look and feel
- White, a lot of air, navy headings, text left-aligned, one generous column.
- Flat panels, no shadows.
- States are small round tags, not coloured text.
- Orange pill only for Post an update; every other button outlined navy.

## Out of scope
- No dashboard, no charts, no metrics tiles.
- No comments or reactions on rows — this is a record, not a feed to engage with.
