## What the wall settles

The portal starts from who is asking, not from what DBS does. Willow's rule is that a need only counts once it is pinned to a named audience and written in that audience's own voice — the question as they would say it out loud. Ten audiences looked like duplication until each was given a concrete question; once named this way, none of them overlap. That is the argument for the format, and it is why the wall comes before any post.

The same rule now applies to every post the areas write. Written for someone outside DBS who does not know our acronyms or what we do: show the market teams what changed and what it means, not what we did, and always say which track and which market it touches. If a post needs explaining, it has failed.

## What is not here yet

The wall names needs; it does not answer them. No post exists yet, for any of the ten, and the seven fields a real post must carry have not been settled — so this page names neither a field nor a track nor a market, rather than inventing ones nobody agreed to. Choosing which need gets answered first, and by which area, is the next sprint's work.

The header carries no navigation because there is nothing else to navigate to yet. When the first area posts, the wall becomes the way in: each card the front of a need, with the posts that answer it behind.
