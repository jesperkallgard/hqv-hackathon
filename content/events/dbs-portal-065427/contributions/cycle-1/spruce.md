These are the needs Dag hears directly when market teams and other outsiders ask DBS questions. One need, one audience, one card — nothing hidden behind it.

```block
type: NeedCard
data:
  items:
    - audience: Market teams
      need: Know what's changing on husqvarna.com next month, and whether it touches their product line.
    - audience: Brand
      need: Know what tests are running right now, and what the results mean for how they talk about the products.
    - audience: IT
      need: Know what's shipping to production, and what dependencies sit on their side.
    - audience: Management
      need: "Know whether the testing programme is moving the metrics that matter: cart additions, lead volume, time on site."
    - audience: New people joining the team
      need: Know what the three tracks actually own, and who to ping when something breaks.
    - audience: EU market teams
      need: Know whether a test result that came out of the US holds true for their region.
    - audience: Automowers team
      need: Know if anything is being tested on the product pages they care about.
    - audience: Support
      need: Know when the Dealer Locator is changing, since they field the questions about it.
    - audience: Green space professionals
      need: Know what new features are coming to the parts of the site they use.
    - audience: Consumers
      need: Know if the My Husqvarna experience is getting easier.
    - audience: Product marketing
      need: Know which segments are seeing which messaging in tests, so they can plan launches around it.
    - audience: Riders team
      need: Know what conversion looks like for their category, so they can brief retailers on it.
```

**Assumption made to hand this in:** no track or market is tagged on any card. The three tracks haven't been named yet in the canon, so pinning a track would have been invented, not real — that's the open question for the next sprint.
