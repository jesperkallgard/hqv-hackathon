Before we write a single post, here's who's actually asking us something — and what they're asking. No acronyms, no digging: one audience, one need, per card.

```block
type: NeedsWall
data:
  items:
    - audience: Market teams
      need: "What's launching on husqvarna.com this quarter, and does it touch my territory?"
    - audience: Brand
      need: "What are you testing about how people see us, and what did the results actually say?"
    - audience: IT
      need: "What's in the release pipeline, so we can plan our sprints around yours?"
    - audience: Management
      need: "Is the money going into testing moving anything the business actually cares about?"
    - audience: New people in DBS
      need: "What does DBS actually own, versus what sits somewhere else in the business?"
    - audience: Dealer Locator owners
      need: "Are you changing how our customers find a dealer?"
    - audience: Paid media
      need: "What landing page experience is live right now, so we're not sending traffic into a test that's about to change under us?"
    - audience: Support
      need: "Are you changing how customers find help?"
    - audience: Segment owners — Professionals, Consumers, Automowers
      need: "Is anything you're testing touching my customer's journey right now?"
    - audience: Finance and anyone with budget
      need: "What's the ROI on the optimisation programme?"
```

All ten are real and none overlap once you name them this way — but we won't get posts written for all ten today. That's what the vote should settle: which of these needs gets answered first.
