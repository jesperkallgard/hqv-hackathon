One list called "What's changed," newest first, is the whole first screen. Every row says what happened, who owns it, and how often it's updated — so the three questions people keep asking a colleague get answered by reading, not asking.

- One list titled "What's changed" is the whole first screen — no tabs, no categories, nothing else above it.
- Newest first, no pagination: if it isn't near the top, it's old enough that asking a colleague is fine.
- Each row is one line plus an owner's name and a date — no body text, no read-more, no detail page.
- There's no search box. Finding out who owns something means reading the owner's name on its most recent row, not searching a directory.
- "What changed," "what's being tested," and "who owns X" are the same kind of row, written as different sentences — not three separate features or tabs.

## Layout
- Navy heading: "What's changed."
- Below it, one flat panel, one column, no shadows, a lot of white space.
- Rows separated by a thin line only — no cards, no icons, no colour coding by type.
- Each row: date, then one sentence, then the owner's name, then how often that owner updates it. In that order, left-aligned.
- No search box, no filter, no category picker anywhere on the screen.

## Real content (example rows, newest first)

1. **4 Feb** — Dealer locator moved to the new map API. — *Priya Nkemelu, updates monthly.*
2. **29 Jan** — Testing two headlines on the homepage hero: "Book a demo" vs "See it in your dealership." — *Tom Reyes, updates weekly.*
3. **15 Jan** — Dealer locator now shows stock levels per dealer. — *Priya Nkemelu, updates monthly.*

Note how rows 1 and 3 answer "who owns the dealer locator" without a separate ownership feature: scan for the most recent row about it, read the name attached. That's the whole mechanism — one row format, three questions answered.

## Tone
Plain, concrete, short sentences. Says what changed and what it meant — not what was done, not marketing language. "Dealer locator moved to the new map API," not "We upgraded our dealer locator experience."

## Out of scope
- No search box, no filters, no tags to click.
- No detail pages behind each row — the one line is the whole post.
- No comments or reactions.
- No separate "ownership" page or directory — ownership lives only on the rows themselves.

## Open
None — this is the whole first screen as agreed.
