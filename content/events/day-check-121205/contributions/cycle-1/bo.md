The first screen answers the three things people keep asking a colleague: what changed this week, what's being tested now, who owns the dealer locator. One list, newest first.

- The whole first screen is one list, newest first — no dashboard, no tabs, no cards.
- Every row answers one of three recurring questions: what changed on the site this week, what's being tested right now, or who owns something.
- A row is one line, an owner's name, and a date. Nothing else sits on it.
- There is no search box. If it isn't findable by reading down the list, it doesn't belong on the list.
- A row only earns its place if it names an owner and says how often it's updated — that's the bar to get on the list, not a detail added afterwards.

## What it is
A single page titled **"What's changed"**. One column, newest entry at the top. That is the entire first screen — no navigation, no side panel, no dashboard tiles.

## What a row looks like
Every row is the same shape, whichever of the three questions it's answering:
- One line of plain text saying what changed or what's true.
- The owner's name.
- A date (when it happened, or when it was last true).

Example rows, in the group's own words:
- "Dealer locator moved to the new map API. Owner: Cleo. Last checked: this week."
- "Homepage hero test is live, testing new vs. old copy. Owner: Bo. Runs until end of month."
- "Checkout error copy changed after complaints. Owner: Bo. This week."

No description field, no tags, no category column. If the type (change / test / ownership) needs to be visible, it comes from what the line says, not from a separate label — do not add a filter chip or a badge system for this. That's the "no search box" point applied one level down: don't build structure the group didn't ask for.

## What it refuses to do
- No search box.
- No filters, no tabs, no "view by owner" toggle.
- No comments, no reactions, no threads under a row.
- Nothing above the list — no welcome text, no summary, no counts.

## Look and feel
Follows the facilitator's direction directly: white, sparse, a lot of air between rows. Navy heading for "What's changed", left-aligned, one column. Flat panels, no shadows, no card borders trying to look like a product page. It should read like a page someone typed, not a page someone designed.

## Open / not decided
Who can add a row, and whether old rows ever drop off the bottom, was not settled this sprint — left open for whoever builds it, or for a later sprint.
