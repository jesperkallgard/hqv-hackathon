What you land on when you click a row — the seven things a colleague needs, one link out, and nothing else to click.

- Opening a row always shows the same seven things in the same order: headline, audience, three-line summary, status/date, owner, cadence, one link onward.
- The summary is three lines and says what changed and what it meant — not the steps it took to do it.
- Exactly one link onward. Not a set of related pages, not a menu — the single next thing to do.
- No comments, no reactions, no edit history. A post is read, not discussed back at.
- The headline says the change the way the reader would say it — 'My Husqvarna login moved', not the name of the project that made it happen.

## What it is
The single-post page a row opens into, from any of the four areas. The list already shows headline, owner and date; this page is where the other four things live: audience, the three-line summary, status, cadence and the one link onward.

## Worked example — real content, not placeholder
- **Headline:** My Husqvarna login moved
- **Audience:** Anyone who logs in with a Husqvarna account
- **Summary (three lines, exactly):**
  1. Login now runs through the new Husqvarna ID system instead of the old site password.
  2. If you signed in before, your email stays the same — you reset your password once.
  3. Nothing else about your account changed.
- **Status:** Live since 3 Nov *(assumed — no date was given us; correct if wrong)*
- **Owner:** Jonas B
- **Cadence:** updated monthly
- **Link onward:** "Reset your password" → the password-reset page

## Layout
One column, flat panel, no shadow. Headline in navy, as a heading, at the top. Audience directly under it in plain text — one line, no label like "Audience:" in front of it, just the sentence. Then the three-line summary, each line its own paragraph, a lot of space between them rather than run together. Status, owner and cadence sit together near the bottom of the panel as a plain line, not a table: "Live since 3 Nov. Owned by Jonas B. Updated monthly." The one link onward sits last, on its own line, styled as a plain link, not a button.

A line back to the area it came from ("Back to What's changed") sits above the headline, small, not competing with it.

## Out of scope
- No comments, no reactions, no "was this useful".
- No history of past versions of the post.
- No second link, no "related posts" block, no tags.
- No search, no breadcrumb beyond the one back-link.

## Why
This is the page the "rest when you open it" in the bar refers to. If a post doesn't have all seven things filled in properly, it hasn't earned its place — this page is where that gets checked, one at a time, not guessed at from the row.
