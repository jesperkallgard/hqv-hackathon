Every post carries seven fields. The list only ever shows three of them — headline, owner, date. The other four wait behind one click.

- Every post has seven fields: headline, audience, a three-line summary, a status or date, an owner by name, how often it is updated, and one link onward.
- The list view shows exactly three of them — headline, owner, date — nothing else, on any of the four areas.
- Opening a post reveals the other four: audience, the three-line summary, status/date detail, cadence and the link onward.
- A post ends in exactly one link onward, never zero and never several — one place to go next, not a list of them.
- The summary is three lines, not a paragraph and not a headline repeated — what changed and what it meant, in that order.

## The seven fields
Every post, in any of the four areas, carries:

1. Headline
2. Audience — who this is for, in a few words
3. A three-line summary — what changed, and what it meant. Not a paragraph.
4. A status or date — either a date something happened, or a live status like "live" / "closed"
5. Owner, by name
6. How often it's updated (cadence)
7. One link onward

## List vs. open
In the list, on any of the four areas, you see three of the seven: headline, owner, date. That matches the row shape already agreed — nothing new there. The other four fields — audience, the three-line summary, status detail, cadence, link onward — only show once you open the post.

## Worked example
Headline: **My Husqvarna login moved**
Audience: Dealers using My Husqvarna
Summary (three lines):
- The login for My Husqvarna moved to a new page.
- Old bookmarks and saved links to the old login now redirect automatically.
- Nothing else about the account changed — same password, same access.

Status/date: Live since 3 Nov
Owner: Jonas B
Cadence: updated monthly
Link onward: Open My Husqvarna login

The audience, summary, status detail and link are our best write-up for this example, not confirmed by Jonas — everything else (headline, owner, cadence) was given to us directly.

## Rule
A post that is missing any of the seven does not go up. This tightens the sprint-two rule (owner + cadence) rather than replacing it.

## Out of scope
No comment thread on a post, no version history, no "read next" suggestions beyond the one link onward.
