No parts had been handed out for this sprint, so we picked the checkout itself: the flow that actually completes the purchase.

The canon's checkout right now is three steps — cart, shipping, payment — before you land on confirmed. We think that's more clicks than a bike needs. Our call: one form, three fields — **email, shipping address, card number** — and a single submit button. No login, no saved addresses, nothing else on the page. Submit takes you straight to a confirmation screen showing the order details back.

The point isn't to add fields, it's to remove steps. Someone who's already decided to buy shouldn't be walked through a wizard to do it.

**Left open:** we agreed on the three fields and the one-page shape, but we ran out of time to settle exactly what "order details" the confirmation screen shows back — just email, or email plus address and a summary of what was bought. That's the next thing to decide.
