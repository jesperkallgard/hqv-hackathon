A front door with four clear cards: What’s changed, What we’re testing, How we do things, and Who owns what. Each card puts a named person up front so an outsider knows where to click and who to ask.

- The page keeps exactly four cards in one column, and the cards are the four areas the room named.
- Each card starts with a named owner, so the first thing you see is the person you can ask.
- Each card shows the last update date and the update rhythm, so freshness is obvious at a glance.
- An outsider can use the four cards to get to the right place without asking a colleague first.
- Nothing on the page is anonymous, and nothing is hidden behind generic labels or vague team wording.

## What the front door is
- A single internal website front door, not a document.
- Keep the existing calm internal-site look: white background, lots of air, navy headings, left-aligned text, flat panels, no shadows.
- Keep the site header, short nav, one generous content column, and footer.
- Buttons stay as fully round pills: orange with white text for the primary action, navy outline for secondary actions.
- Statuses stay as small round tags, not coloured words.

## The four cards
- The page has exactly four cards in one vertical column.
- The cards are:
  1. What’s changed
  2. What we’re testing
  3. How we do things
  4. Who owns what
- The cards should read like clear entry points for an outsider.
- Each card must make it obvious where to click next.

## Ownership
- Ownership is the priority.
- Every card names a person up front.
- The page must not feel anonymous or team-buried.
- The owner should be visible before any detail or summary.

## Freshness
- Each card shows the last update date.
- Each card also shows how often it is updated.
- Freshness should be easy to scan without opening anything.

## Content and tone
- Use plain, concrete language.
- Short sentences.
- Say what changed and what it meant, not what was done.
- No marketing language.

## Out of scope
- No extra sections beyond the four cards.
- No second layout for mobile or desktop.
- No anonymous summaries or placeholder copy.
- No extra routing structure beyond the four obvious paths.

## Notes
- This hand-in follows the four areas the group named and keeps ownership as the strongest signal.
- Use the group members as authored by the sprint system.
