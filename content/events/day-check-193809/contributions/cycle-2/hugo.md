A calm front door for an outsider who needs the answer fast. Four cards, one column, with staleness and ownership obvious on first glance.

- The front door is four cards in one column: What's changed, What we're testing, How we do things, and Who owns what.
- Each card is a clear click target that takes you straight to that area, so an outsider knows where to go without asking.
- Every card shows the last update date up front, and the update is easy to compare across the four cards.
- Each card also shows who last touched it, so stale areas stand out immediately.
- The page stays simple and quiet: white space, flat cards, no shadows, and short plain labels.

Build the landing page as a real internal website, not a document. Keep the existing site name, header, short nav, and footer from the canon, but make the first view a one-column set of four cards only.

The four cards are:
- What's changed
- What we're testing
- How we do things
- Who owns what

Each card must:
- be fully clickable
- show the section name as the main label
- show the last update date prominently
- show who last touched it
- make staleness obvious at a glance

This sprint is about the front door only. Do not add new sections or a deeper content page here.

Keep the look aligned to the brief: lots of white space, flat cards, navy headings, text left-aligned, no shadows, and buttons only as rounded pills where needed. Use short, concrete copy. The page should feel calm and internal, not promotional.

Use the canon as the starting point, but simplify the landing view around these four areas so the first thing people see is where to click and what is stale.

Out of scope: changing the underlying post content, redesigning the owners directory, or adding new navigation beyond the four areas.
