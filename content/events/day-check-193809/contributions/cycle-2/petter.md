A calm front door with four cards and no extra clutter. An outsider can scan the page and know where to click first.

- The page shows four cards only: What’s changed, What we’re testing, How we do things, and Who owns what.
- Each card is a direct route into the part of the site it names, so the front door stays small and useful.
- The cards sit in one column on a white page with lots of air, navy headings, and left-aligned text.
- The front door does not add a separate directory or extra feed items; the cards do the work instead.
- Each card shows the last update date so people can tell what is current at a glance.

Build the front door as a real internal website, not a document. Keep the header with the site name and short nav, then one generous column of four flat cards, then a footer.

Use the four names exactly as the card titles:
- What’s changed
- What we’re testing
- How we do things
- Who owns what

The cards should read as the main navigation. They must be the only visible content on the landing page apart from the header and footer.

Content rules:
- Show the last update date on every card.
- Do not add a longer owner directory or feed preview to the landing page.
- Do not add extra sections, charts, or promotional copy.
- Keep the copy plain and concrete.
- Use short sentences.
- The page should feel restrained: fewer things, each doing more work.

Look and feel:
- White background.
- Lots of air.
- Navy headings.
- Left-aligned text.
- Flat panels with no shadows.
- Fully round pill buttons only if any action appears.
- Orange with white text for the primary action.
- Navy outlined pills for secondary actions.
- Small round tags for states, not coloured words.

Out of scope:
- No extra front-door modules.
- No document-like layout.
- No additional copy blocks below the cards.
