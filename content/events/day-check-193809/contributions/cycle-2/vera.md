A front door for people who need the answer fast and do not want to ask a colleague. Four cards, one column, one screen: what changed, what we’re testing, how we do things, and who owns what.

- Show only four cards on the front door, in one vertical column.
- Use the four exact sections: What’s changed, What we’re testing, How we do things, Who owns what.
- Put the last update date on every card so freshness is visible at a glance.
- Keep the whole answer on one screen with no scrolling on a normal laptop view.
- Make it clear where to click next, so an outsider can find the right section without asking for help.

## What the front door has to do
- Present the site as a real internal website, not a document.
- The front door is the first thing people see.
- It uses four cards only, one column, in this order:
  1. What’s changed
  2. What we’re testing
  3. How we do things
  4. Who owns what
- Each card includes a last update date.
- The page must answer the common question in one screen, without scrolling on a normal laptop view.
- An outsider should understand which card to click from the card titles alone.

## Content
- Keep the section names exactly as the group wrote them.
- The page is for people who need the answer fast and do not want to ask a colleague.
- The cards should lead into the existing internal content, not invent new areas.

## Look and feel
- White background, lots of air.
- Navy headings.
- Left-aligned text.
- Flat panels, no shadows.
- Buttons stay fully round pill shapes.
- Primary button: orange with white text.
- Secondary buttons: outlined in navy.
- States are small round tags, not coloured words.

## Out of scope
- No extra navigation sections on the front door.
- No second column.
- No long intro or marketing copy.
- No scrolling-first layout.
- No new content areas beyond the four cards.

## Notes
- This is the front door only.
- The idea is speed and clarity: one screen, four clear ways in, each with freshness visible.
- If the build needs more detail for labels, keep them plain and concrete.
