The front door is four cards in one column, so an outsider can click straight to the answer they need. It is for people from other teams who do not know our vocabulary.

- The page shows four cards only: What's changed, What we're testing, How we do things, and Who owns what.
- The cards sit in one column and each one is the obvious click target for its area.
- Each card shows the last update date so stale content is easy to spot.
- The card labels use plain outsider language, not internal shorthand.
- The page does not try to explain the whole site; it just gets people to the right place fast.

## Content
- Use the four section names exactly as given: What's changed, What we're testing, How we do things, Who owns what.
- Show the last update date on every card.
- Keep the copy plain and concrete. No marketing language.
- Write for someone from another team who needs no vocabulary from us.

## Layout and feel
- One generous column.
- Flat cards with no shadows.
- White background, lots of air, navy headings, left-aligned text.
- The front door should feel like a real internal website, not a document.

## Scope
- No extra sections.
- No extra explanation before the cards.
- No clever labels or internal shorthand.
- The page's job is to route people, not to tell the whole story.
