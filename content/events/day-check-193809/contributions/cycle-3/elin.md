A post for people outside the team who need the new login path without asking a colleague. It shows what changed, who owns it, how often it is updated, and where to go next.

- The post opens in full and reads like a real internal page, not a document.
- The list view shows the headline, the owner, and the date.
- Inside the post, the page shows the audience, a three-line summary, a status or date, the owner, the update rhythm, and one onward link.
- The owner is Jonas B and the post is updated monthly.
- The post is written for an outsider who does not know our vocabulary and needs the answer fast.

## Content and tone
- Use short, plain sentences.
- Say what changed and what it meant.
- No marketing language.
- Keep the wording concrete and easy to scan.

- Header with the site name and short navigation.
- One generous column.
- Footer.
- White background.
- Lots of air.
- Navy headings.
- Left-aligned text.

- Buttons are fully round pills.
