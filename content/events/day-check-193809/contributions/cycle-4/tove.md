One internal site that helps people find the answer fast without asking a colleague. It shows the latest changes, who owns them, and what they need to do next.

- A plain header gives the site name and three routes in: the feed, who owns what, and how we work.
- The feed is the main view, with newest changes first and each row showing the owner, the date, and the one action it asks for.
- Every entry is written to say what changed and what it means, not to describe the process behind it.
- States are small round tags, not coloured words, and the page stays calm, white, and navy with orange used only for the one primary button.
- The site has no dashboard and no charts; the footer names who keeps it up to date.

## Prototype scope
Keep the site as a real internal website, not a document. One header, one generous content column, and a footer.

## Content and structure
- Site name: **What's Changed**.
- Header navigation: **the feed**, **who owns what**, **how we work**.
- Main feed is the first thing people see.
- Feed order: newest first.
- Each feed row shows:
  - the change title
  - the owner
  - the date
  - the one action it wants from you
- Every entry must say what changed and what it meant, not what was done.
- Footer text should say who keeps the site.

## Look and feel
- White background.
- Lots of air.
- Navy headings.
- Left-aligned text.
- Flat panels only.
- No shadows anywhere.
- Buttons are fully round pills.
- The one primary button is orange with white text.
- Other buttons are outlined in navy.
- States are small round tags, not coloured words.
- Calm, plain, concrete tone.

## Out of scope
- No dashboard.
- No charts.
- No marketing language.
- No extra visual chrome.

## Assumptions
- The page is already an internal site and should keep that character.
- The feed is the main way in; the other routes are secondary.
