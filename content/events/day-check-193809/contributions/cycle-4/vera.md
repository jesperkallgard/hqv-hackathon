A calm internal site that gets people to the answer fast without asking a colleague. The feed stays first, with three clear routes above it and the owner and date on every row.

- The feed is the first thing people see, because the answer should be on one screen.
- The header keeps three clear routes in: the feed, who owns what, and how we work.
- Each row shows the change, the owner, and the date so nobody has to hunt for context.
- The page stays a simple internal site: white, navy headings, lots of air, and no dashboard or charts.
- Orange is reserved for the one primary action, with pills and small round status tags keeping the rest quiet.

## What the page has to do
- Keep the site as a real internal website, not a document.
- Put the feed at the front so a person can get their answer in one screen without scrolling.
- Keep the three routes in the header: the feed, who owns what, and how we work.
- Show the feed newest first.
- Keep the footer with who keeps the site.

## Content to show
- Site name: `What's Changed`.
- Feed items must show:
  - what changed this week
  - what is being tested
  - who owns the dealer locator
- Every row must include an owner and a date.
- Keep the owner and update rhythm visible for each post.
- Use the current copy style: plain, concrete, short sentences.

## Look and feel
- White background.
- A lot of air.
- Navy headings.
- Text left-aligned.
- Flat panels only, no shadows anywhere.
- One orange primary button with white text.
- Other buttons outlined in navy.
- States are small round tags, not coloured words.
- Calm and compact enough that the answer sits on one screen.

## Deliberately out of scope
- No dashboard.
- No charts.
- No extra sections beyond the feed, the owner view, and how we work.
- No marketing language.
- No new content model beyond the rows and their owner/date fields.

## Notes
- The page should read like a working internal site.
- Speed matters most: the feed must be visible immediately, before any deeper navigation.
