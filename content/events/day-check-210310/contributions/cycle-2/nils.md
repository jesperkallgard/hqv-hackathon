A front door with four cards for the four areas people ask about most. It gets an outsider to the right place in one screen, without scrolling.

- The page shows four cards in one column: What’s changed, What we’re testing, How we do things, and Who owns what.
- Each card is a direct way in, so an outsider knows where to click without reading a long page.
- Every card shows its last update date on the card itself.
- The first card answers the most common question fastest, so the useful answer is visible above the fold.
- The page stays a single-screen front door and does not add extra sections, feeds, or dashboards.

Build the front door as a real internal website, not a document.

### Structure
- Header with the site name and a short navigation.
- One generous column.
- Four cards in one vertical stack.
- Footer.

### The four cards
- What’s changed
- What we’re testing
- How we do things
- Who owns what

### Content and behaviour
- Each card must read as a place to click, not a summary block.
- Each card must show its last update date on the card itself.
- The layout should let someone get to an answer in one screen without scrolling.
- Keep the first card most prominent so the fastest route to the answer is clear.
- Do not add extra sections, long intro copy, feeds, or dashboard-style modules.

### Look and feel
- White background.
- A lot of air.
- Navy headings.
- Left-aligned text.
- Flat panels, no shadows.
- Buttons are fully round pills.
- Primary button: orange with white text.
- Other buttons: outlined in navy.
- States are small round tags, not coloured words.

### Tone
- Plain and concrete.
- Short sentences.
- Say what changed and what it meant.
- No marketing language.

### What this must avoid
- No document-like layout.
- No extra navigation tree.
- No scrolling to find the main answer.
- No extra analytics or dashboard layer.
- No unrelated sections beyond the four agreed areas.
