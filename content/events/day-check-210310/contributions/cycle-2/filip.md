Four cards give people the fastest path to the answer. The front door is for people who need to know what changed, what is being tested, how we do things, or who owns what without asking around.

- The front door is four cards in one column: What’s changed, What we’re testing, How we do things, and Who owns what.
- Each card shows the last update date so people can see what is fresh at a glance.
- Each card tells you the one next action it wants from you, so the page does not stop at a label.
- An outsider can read the four cards and know exactly where to click next.
- The page stays a real internal website: header, nav, one content column, and footer, with no dashboard or charts.

Build the front door for the existing What’s Changed site as one internal page.

Must use these four card names exactly:
- What’s changed
- What we’re testing
- How we do things
- Who owns what

Layout and feel:
- Real internal website, not a document.
- Header with the site name and a short navigation.
- One generous column for the content.
- Footer at the bottom.
- White background.
- A lot of air.
- Navy headings.
- Text left-aligned.
- Panels are flat.
- No shadows anywhere.
- Buttons are fully round pills.
- Primary button: orange with white text.
- Secondary buttons: outlined in navy.
- Statuses are small round tags, not coloured words.

Card behaviour:
- Exactly four cards on the front door.
- Each card includes the last update date.
- Each card includes one clear action it wants from the user.
- The action should be obvious from the card, not hidden.
- The four cards should make it clear where to go next.
- An outsider should be able to read the four cards and know where to click.

Content and purpose:
- This page is the front door to the site, not a dashboard.
- It should help people find the answer without asking a colleague.
- Keep the tone plain and concrete.
- Short sentences.
- Say what changed and what it meant.
- No marketing language.
- Do not add charts.
- Do not add a dashboard.
- Do not add extra navigation systems.

Ownership and freshness:
- Every card must still support the site rule that a post earns its place if it saves somebody from asking a colleague.
- The page should keep ownership visible, and each card should connect to the people or area behind it.
- The last update date is required on every card so freshness is obvious.
