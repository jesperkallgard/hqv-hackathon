A single opened post for people who need the change fast. It says what moved, who owns it, and when it changes next.

- The list stays lean and shows only the headline, the owner, and the date.
- Opening the post reveals the audience, a three-line summary, a status or date, the owner, the update rhythm, and one onward link.
- The post is owned by Jonas B and updated monthly.
- The page stays a real internal website with one generous column, a header, and a footer.
- The design stays white, airy, and restrained, with navy headings, flat panels, and round pill buttons.

Make the first post use this exact title: My Husqvarna login moved.

Use Jonas B as the owner.

Set the update frequency to monthly.

The page is still one post, opened: the list shows only the headline, owner, and date; opening it shows the rest.

The opened post must include:
- audience
- a three-line summary
- a status or date
- owner name
- update frequency
- one link onward

Keep the writing plain and concrete. Say what changed and what it meant. No marketing language.

Keep the site looking like a real internal website:
- header with the site name and short navigation
- one generous column
- footer
- white background
- lots of air
- navy headings
- left-aligned text
- flat panels, no shadows
- fully round pill buttons
- orange with white text for the primary action
- outlined navy for the rest
- small round tags for states, not coloured words

Out of scope:
- no feed
- no dashboard
- no extra layer before opening the post
- no extra motion or chrome
