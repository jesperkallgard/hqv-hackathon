The Husqvarna login moved to a new place. This post tells people where it went, who owns it, and when it changes.

- It gives the new login location in the first screen.
- It shows the owner, Jonas B, so people know who to ask.
- It says the post is updated monthly.
- It keeps the answer short enough to read without scrolling.
- It links onward to the next place to go if the login still does not work.

## What this post contains
- Title: **My Husqvarna login moved**
- Owner: **Jonas B**
- Update frequency: **Monthly**
- The post must answer the move in one screen.
- It must show the new login location first.
- It must include one onward link for the next step if the login still does not work.

## Content
- Use plain words.
- Say what changed and what it meant.
- Keep the three-line summary short and concrete.
- Include a status or date.
- Include the owner name and update frequency visibly on the opened post.
- In the list view, show the headline, the owner, and the date.

## Look and feel
- Real internal website, not a document.
- White, lots of air, navy headings, left-aligned text.
- Flat panels, no shadows.
- Orange fully round primary button with white text.
- Other buttons are outlined navy pills.
- Statuses are small round tags, not coloured words.

## Out of scope
- No extra feed.
- No dashboard.
- No multi-step flow.
- No marketing language.
- No hidden second post.

## Notes
- The page should stay buildable as a single opened post.
- The content should help someone avoid asking a colleague.
