A calm internal site that shows what changed, who owns it, and how to work without asking around. It is for people who need the answer fast and want a plain site that feels like a real internal tool.

- The site opens with a header, the site name, and short navigation to the feed, who owns what, and how we work.
- The feed is the main view, showing what changed, what is being tested, and who owns things like the dealer locator.
- Every post names an owner and how often it is updated, so the answer is on the page.
- The site ends with a footer that says who keeps it.
- It stays restrained: white, lots of air, navy headings, flat panels, left-aligned text, and one orange pill for the primary action.

Build the whole site as a single internal website called **What's Changed**.

### Content
- Header with:
  - site name
  - short navigation
    - Feed
    - Who owns what
    - How we work
- Feed as the main content.
- Feed items should cover:
  - what changed this week
  - what is being tested
  - who owns the dealer locator and similar things
- Every post must include:
  - an owner
  - how often it is updated
- Footer with a line saying who keeps the site.

### Structure
- One generous content column.
- Feed-first layout.
- The page should read as the whole site, not just a section.

### Look and feel
- Real internal website, not a document.
- White background.
- Lots of air.
- Navy headings.
- Left-aligned text.
- Flat panels only.
- No shadows anywhere.
- Buttons are fully round pills.
- Primary action: orange with white text.
- Other actions: outlined in navy.
- States are small round tags, not coloured words.
- Calm and restrained.

### Tone
- Plain and concrete.
- Short sentences.
- Say what changed and what it meant.
- No marketing language.

### Scope
- No dashboard.
- No charts.
- Keep the page sparse.
- Every element should help someone avoid asking a colleague.

### Notes
- The site should feel like an internal reference people trust.
- The feed, owners, and how we work should all feel like parts of one coherent site, not separate pages.
