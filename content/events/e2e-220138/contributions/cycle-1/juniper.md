The first screen asks one question.

It does not guess. It does not show options. It asks.

Guessing wrong costs more than asking. A wrong guess sends someone down the wrong path and they have to notice, back out, and start over. A question costs them one answer.

So the first screen's job is not to explain the product. Its job is to ask the one thing that decides what happens next.
