## What the room decided

The site is called **DBS 4 President** (Team Jesper) and it is built as **What's Changed** (Team Bo): one generous column, one newest-first feed, three ways in — Feed, Who owns what, How we work.

The two ideas disagreed on the top of the page. Jesper wanted a KPI panel and three entry points; Bo argued the whole site is one feed — "not a dashboard, no charts, nothing to configure". The page goes Jesper's way: four flat numbers, no charts, nothing to configure. Bo's objection stands and is worth holding to — the feed is the point, and the four numbers must never turn this into a status report written upward. If they start being read that way, they come off.

Explore, Optimize and Scale are phases, not topics. They are the entry points and the tag on every row, so choosing one filters the feed in place. They are not in the navigation.

## What we filled in

The room decided the name, the three nav items, the four metrics, the phases, the tag treatment and the rule that every post names an owner and a cadence. Everything with a value on it — the numbers, the eight posts, the seven owners, the five working rules, the footer — we wrote so there was a whole page to argue with. All people on it are invented. The room left open what the orange button does (we called it *Post an update*, per Bo's candidate), who exactly the site is for, and what differs in content between the three phases beyond the tag; "How we work" was out of scope this sprint and is built out here rather than left blank.
