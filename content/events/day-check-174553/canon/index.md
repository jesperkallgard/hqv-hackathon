## What the room decided

This sprint keeps the front door and adds the opened shape of one post beneath it. Team Dag defined the opened entry itself: one change to act on, with the headline exactly **My Husqvarna login moved**, owned by **Jonas B**, updated **monthly**, and carrying the extra context a colleague would ask for only when opened. Team Bo set the page frame that still holds it: the site is called **What's Changed**, with a header, short navigation, one generous column, and a footer that says who keeps the site.

The tension was about what the feed is for. *My Husqvarna login moved* argued for the difference between the list row and the opened post, keeping the list short. *What's Changed* argued for the feed as the main view, newest first. The page keeps that spine and opens one entry inside it, rather than replacing the front door with a separate post page.

## What we filled in

The room gave the site name, navigation labels, the exact post headline, owner, cadence, and the fields the opened entry must carry. We supplied the feed rows, dates, statuses, audience, summary, onward links, and footer keeper so the page reads as a complete internal site.
