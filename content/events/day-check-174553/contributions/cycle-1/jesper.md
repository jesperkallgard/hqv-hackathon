A homepage built around four KPIs at the top and three entry points below — Explore, Optimize, Scale — which are also the phases every initiative moves through and the status tag on every post.

- The top of the page shows a KPI panel with four metrics: what's been deployed, number of tests, money the site brings in, and number of visitors.
- Below the KPIs sit three entry points: Explore, Optimize, and Scale.
- Explore, Optimize, and Scale are phases an initiative goes through in its development, not topic categories like IT or HR.
- The same words — Explore, Optimize, Scale — are also the small round status tag on each post, so clicking an entry point just shows all posts currently in that phase.
- The site's name, shown in the header, is 'DBS 4 President'.
