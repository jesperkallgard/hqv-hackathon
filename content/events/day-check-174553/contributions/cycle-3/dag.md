The login moved. Jonas B owns the note and updates it monthly. Open it to see the full change, who it affects, and where to go next.

- The post is a single opened entry, not a list item. It carries one change people need to act on, not a roundup.
- The headline is exactly My Husqvarna login moved.
- The owner is Jonas B, named on the post, and the update cadence is monthly.
- In the list view the user sees the headline, the owner, and the update date. The full entry is only visible when opened.
- The opened post includes the extra detail a colleague would ask for: the audience, a three-line summary, a status or date, and one onward link.

## Opened post shape
The first post is titled exactly **My Husqvarna login moved**.

It is owned by **Jonas B**.

It is updated **monthly**.

## What the list shows
In the front list, the user sees:
- the headline
- the owner name
- the update date / cadence

Nothing else needs to show in the list row.

## What the opened post contains
The opened post contains seven fields:
1. Headline
2. Audience
3. Three-line summary
4. Status or date
5. Owner by name
6. How often it is updated
7. One link onward

The opened view is the place for the extra context; the list stays short.

## Look and feel
Follow the day's direction:
- real internal site, not a document
- white, airy layout
- navy headings
- left-aligned text
- flat panels, no shadows
- pill buttons, orange for the primary action, outlined navy for the rest
- status shown as small round tags, not coloured words

## Out of scope
No extra fields on the list row.
No marketing copy.
No second post format.
No dashboard controls on the front door.

## Content assumptions
Only the headline, owner and update cadence were given for this post. The audience, summary, status/date wording and onward link text are intentionally left for the build to fill from the site's content model unless the room decides otherwise.
