The front door is four cards in one column and nothing above them: What's changed, What we're testing, How we do things, Who owns what. Each card shows only its name and when it was last updated.

- The front door has exactly four cards, in one column — no banner, no KPI panel, no feed preview sitting above them.
- The four cards are named exactly: What's changed, What we're testing, How we do things, Who owns what. No fifth card, no renaming, no shortening.
- They run in that order top to bottom — what's changed first, because that's the one people check most.
- Each card carries only its name and the date it was last updated. No blurb, no owner, no post count on the card itself — that detail lives one click in.
- Clicking a card is the only thing to do on this page. Nothing to configure, nothing to filter, nothing to search on the front door itself.

## The four cards, in order
1. What's changed
2. What we're testing
3. How we do things
4. Who owns what

Exact casing, exact wording — these are the names, not placeholders. No fifth card is ever added without a decision to do so.

## What's on each card
Just two things: the name, and the date it was last updated — e.g. "Last updated 3 days ago". Nothing else. No description line, no owner name, no count of posts inside. The card is a door, not a preview.

## Layout and look (per the day's direction)
- One generous column, white background, a lot of air between the four cards.
- Card titles in navy, left-aligned.
- Panels are flat — no shadows, no gradients, no borders that fake depth.
- Whole page sits under the header (site name + short nav) and above the footer, same as the rest of the site.

## Out of scope, on purpose
- No content preview, teaser text, or owner name on the front door cards — that would make this a second feed instead of a door.
- Search, filters, and sorting do not belong on this page.

## Not decided
The exact wording of the date stamp ("Last updated 3 days ago" vs. a plain date like "12 May") was not agreed — we picked the relative form as a placeholder because it reads plainer, but the group did not settle it.
