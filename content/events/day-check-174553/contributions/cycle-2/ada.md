The front page opens with four KPIs, an AI chat for finding a specific initiative, and three entry points — Explore, Optimize, Scale — that double as the status tag on every post.

- The site is called DBS 4 President and that name sits in the header.
- The top of the page shows four KPIs: what's been deployed, number of tests, revenue the site brings in, and number of visitors.
- Below the KPIs sits an AI chat, not a search box — this is the first thing used when looking for a specific initiative by name (e.g. 'Dealer locator').
- The AI chat answers by saying what's being done and what the current status is, rather than just linking to a post.
- Explore, Optimize, and Scale are the three entry points on the front page and also the status tag shown on every post, so an entry point just shows all posts currently in that phase.

Build: header with site name 'DBS 4 President' and short navigation; below it a KPI panel with four figures (deployed, number of tests, revenue, visitors — real numbers still needed); below that an AI chat (not a search field) positioned right after the KPIs; below that three entry points labelled Explore, Optimize, Scale, which are the same words used as the small round status tag on each post. Clicking an entry point filters the feed to posts currently tagged with that phase. The AI chat's job: when asked about a named initiative (e.g. 'Dealer locator'), it answers in plain language what is being done and what the current status is — it does not just return a link. Look and feel per the facilitator's direction: real internal site not a document, one generous column, white with a lot of air, navy headings, left-aligned text, flat panels with no shadows, fully round pill buttons (orange with white text for the primary action, navy outline for the rest), and status shown as small round tags rather than coloured words. Voice: plain and concrete, short sentences, says what changed and what it meant. Open / not yet decided: what the orange primary button is called and does (e.g. 'Nytt inlägg' was floated but not confirmed); whether the AI chat's 'no result found' behaviour has been defined; who exactly the site is for (which team or department within DBS) is still unanswered; whether posts also carry an owner name and update frequency, as the general 'worth keeping' rule requires, has not been discussed for this front page; exact KPI figures/units are not specified, only the four categories.
