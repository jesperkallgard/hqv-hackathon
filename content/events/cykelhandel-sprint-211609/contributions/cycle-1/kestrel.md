One page, one job: is the $8,500 Domane SLR 9 actually worth it over a $3,000 gravel bike, or is the money going somewhere the rider won't feel?

We put the frame geometry side by side so the buyer sees numbers, not adjectives. We named the real differences — frame weight, the IsoSpeed decoupler, the groupset tier — and then said plainly who *shouldn't* pay for them. Three placeholder tiles stand in for real customer photos of the bike in mud and on rock, because a bike this expensive earns its price in bad conditions, not on a showroom floor. Everything else — reviews, color pickers, "you might also like" — is cut. It's one decision, made once, well.
