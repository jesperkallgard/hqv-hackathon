One bike: Trailhead 3 Mountain Bike.

Three sizes: S, M, L. Each has its own price adjustment and its own stock count, shown honestly — including when a size is low or out of stock.

Three colors: Black, Red, Blue. Each has its own price adjustment. Picking a color swaps the product image.

Click a size, click a color. Price updates immediately — base price plus size adjustment plus color adjustment. No cart, no checkout, no spec-dump. Just: what it looks like, what it costs, whether you can get it.

No reviews, no lifestyle copy, no taglines. Image, size, color, price, stock. That's the whole page.

**Still open:** we asked the builder to wire this up as a real clickable component — buttons for size and color that swap the image and update the price text live. The build didn't land before the clock ran out, so this sprint hands in the settled design (bike, sizes, colors, price adjustments, stock rule) rather than the working component. Next step is building it for real off this spec.
