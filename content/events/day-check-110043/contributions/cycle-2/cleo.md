The home page is four cards in one column, named exactly What's changed, What we're testing, How we do things, Who owns what — nothing else on it.

- Four cards, one column, in this order: What's changed, What we're testing, How we do things, Who owns what. Not alphabetical, not most-recently-updated first — that order, fixed.
- Each card shows its name and the date it last changed. Nothing else — no description line, no icon, no count of how many posts are inside.
- The four names are the whole explanation. An outsider reads them and knows where to click, so no card gets a strapline trying to sell what's behind it.
- It is a front door, not a menu — no dropdown, no hover state revealing sub-items, no fifth card added later without the room deciding that.
- A card's date is the date something on that page last changed, not the date the page was built — if nothing has changed behind a card, its date doesn't move.

## What it is

The site's home page. One column, four flat panels stacked top to bottom, no shadows, a lot of air between them. Navy for each card's name, left-aligned.

## The four cards, in order, exact names

1. **What's changed** — last updated 14 Oct (matches the newest row on that page today).
2. **What we're testing** — last updated 13 Oct.
3. **How we do things** — last updated 1 Oct.
4. **Who owns what** — last updated 11 Oct.

Assumption: the dates for cards 2–4 are placeholders picked to look plausible against What's changed, which already exists in the canon — nobody has built those three pages yet, so there's nothing real to date them against. Swap them for real dates once each page has content.

## Card content

Each card is just its name, and the date, small, underneath or beside it. No description, no summary of what's inside, no icon. If the name doesn't already tell an outsider where to click, the fix is a better name, not an explanation added to the card.

## What it refuses to do

- No fifth card. If something doesn't fit one of the four, it doesn't go on the front door — that's a decision for the room, not something a card grows on its own.
- No dropdown or hover menu revealing what's inside a card before it's clicked.
- No ranking or highlighting one card over another — same size, same weight, same treatment, in the fixed order above.
- No "last visited" or personalised state. Every reader sees the same four cards.

## Open

What's changed already exists. What we're testing, How we do things and Who owns what don't have pages yet — this hand-in is the front door only, not the content behind the other three cards. That's the next thing to build, and it isn't this group's to invent unasked.
