One page linking to the four areas — What's changed, What we're testing, How we do things, Who owns what — each a card with a one-line job and the date it last moved. No fifth card, ever.

- The homepage is four cards in one column, in this fixed order: What's changed, What we're testing, How we do things, Who owns what — no icons, no grouping, no fifth card.
- Each card shows only the name, one line saying what question it answers, and the date it last changed — never a preview of what's inside the area.
- An area with nothing published yet still gets its card; it shows "Nothing here yet" instead of a date, so the four-way map is true from day one, not just once all four are full.
- The four names are fixed text, not something a post can rename or a team can relabel — a post that doesn't fit one of the four is a sign the site needs a fifth area, not a reason to bend one of these.
- A card links straight into that area's list. There is no hub page or extra landing screen between the front door and the content.

## What it is
The homepage. One column, four flat panels, top to bottom, in this exact order — the order is part of the decision, not just the names:

1. **What's changed** — "What shipped, and what it meant." — 14 Oct
2. **What we're testing** — "What's running as an experiment, and which way it's leaning." — Nothing here yet
3. **How we do things** — "The rules and conventions teams follow, written down once instead of explained again." — Nothing here yet
4. **Who owns what** — "Which team owns which part of the site, so you know who to ask." — Nothing here yet

Each card is: the name (navy heading), the one-line job under it in plain body text, and the date right-aligned or trailing, quiet — same weight as the date treatment already agreed on the What's changed list. No card copy beyond that one line. No thumbnail, no "3 updates this week" counter, no preview of the latest row.

Cards that have nothing behind them yet still render — same size, same style — with "Nothing here yet" where the date goes. That's a real state, not an error, because the room agreed on four areas before three of them have content.

## Look and feel
Follows the direction as already set: white, sparse, a lot of air between the four panels, navy headings, left-aligned, one column, no shadows. This page especially should look written, not designed — it's a list of four doors, not a dashboard.

## What it refuses to do
No search, no filter, no "recently viewed", no personalisation, no icons standing in for the names. The four names are the whole map — that's the point of fixing them at four, so this page never grows a fifth thing bolted onto the side.

## Left open
Bo and Dag gave us the four names and the instruction to write it up — the exact one-line job description under each name, and the "Nothing here yet" wording, were chosen here to fill the gap, not confirmed word-for-word by the group. Worth checking back on the wording, not the shape.

What's changed already has its own page (canon). This front door links to it; it doesn't change what's on it.
