Every post on the site — a change, a test, a rule, an ownership answer — is built the same way: seven things, three of them visible in the list, the rest only once you open it.

- Every post has seven fields: headline, audience, a three-line summary, a status, an owner, how often it's updated, and one link onward.
- In a list you see only three of those: headline, owner, date. The rest waits behind the open.
- Date and status are separate fields. Date is always there. Status is an optional word, only added when there's a state to track — running, closed.
- Opening a post is where audience, the summary, the status and the link onward show up — never before.
- The link onward always points somewhere real: another post, a doc, a ticket — never a ‘read more’ that goes nowhere.

## The seven fields
1. **Headline** — the fact, in one line.
2. **Audience** — who this is for, in a word or two (e.g. "Everyone", "Support team"). Text only, not a filter or a tag you can click.
3. **Three-line summary** — what changed and what it meant. Not what was done.
4. **Status or date** — two different things, not one. The date is always there: when it happened, or when it was last confirmed true. Status is a separate, optional word, only added when the thing has a state worth tracking (a test that's "Running", a rule that's "Closed"). A one-off change gets a date and no status.
5. **Owner** — a name, not a team, wherever a name exists.
6. **Cadence** — how often it's updated ("Updated monthly", "Confirmed each quarter").
7. **One link onward** — to the real thing: the login system, the test's dashboard, the doc. Never a dead "read more".

## List vs. open
A list row (on any of the four doors, or inside "What's changed") shows exactly three things: headline, owner, date. Nothing else leaks into the list — no summary preview, no status badge, no audience tag. Opening the post is what reveals audience, the summary, the status (if it has one), and the link onward.

- **Headline:** My Husqvarna login moved *(given)*
- **Owner:** Jonas B *(given)*
- **Cadence:** Updated monthly *(given)*
- **Audience:** Everyone *(assumed — not confirmed by the group)*
- **Summary:** Login now happens on a separate page instead of a popup. Existing passwords still work. Anyone locked out should use "forgot password", not raise a ticket. *(assumed)*
- **Date / status:** 18 March, no status — it's a one-off change, not something with an ongoing state. *(assumed)*
- **Link onward:** to the login page itself. *(assumed)*

## Look and voice
Flat panel, no shadow, navy headline, left-aligned, one column — same as the rest of the site. The open view is still plain text with the seven fields in a fixed order, not a form and not a card grid.

## Out of scope
No tagging, no filtering by audience, no colour-coded status badges. Status is a plain word next to the date, not a pill.

## Not decided
Whether "audience" is ever shown in the list (this group says no — list stays three fields). What happens when a post has no sensible link onward — not addressed.
