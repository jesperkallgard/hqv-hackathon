The front door is four cards in one column — What's changed, What we're testing, How we do things, Who owns what — each showing its last update and a one-line teaser, so an outsider knows where to click and gets a partial answer before they even do.

- The front door has exactly four cards, one column, in this fixed order: What's changed, What we're testing, How we do things, Who owns what.
- Each card shows its title, the date of its last update, and a one-line teaser of the newest entry — so you get an answer without clicking through.
- Clicking a card opens that area's own list. The front door itself holds no rows of its own, only the four teasers.
- No fifth card, no subcategories, and no search across all four — an outsider reads four titles and knows exactly where to click.
- "What's changed" stops being the whole homepage and becomes one of the four doors; its list moves under its own card.

## What it is
One column, four flat panels, no shadows, stacked in this order: What's changed, What we're testing, How we do things, Who owns what. This page replaces the old homepage — the "What's changed" list from sprint 1 no longer sits on the front door itself, it sits behind its own card.

## A card
Title, last-update date, and one line of plain text — the newest entry from that area, in the same voice as the list it comes from. No icon, no image, no count badge.

Using the room's own rows from sprint 1 as the teaser content for three of the four cards:
- **What's changed** — "Dealer locator now shows a store's opening hours instead of just its address." — 14 March
- **What we're testing** — "Testing a new checkout flow on 10% of traffic." — 12 March
- **Who owns what** — "Dealer locator is owned by Bo. Updated monthly." — 11 March
- **How we do things** — no content exists for this area yet. This sprint decides the door exists, not what's behind it. Leave the teaser blank or "Nothing posted yet" rather than inventing one.

## What it refuses to do
- No fifth card and no way to add one from this page — four areas, no more.
- No search box across the four areas.
- No rows shown directly on the front door beyond the one teaser per card — the front door is a signpost, not a fifth list.

## A note for the room
Sprint 1 decided "What's changed" should be one mixed list on purpose, refusing categories so a change, a test, and an ownership note could sit side by side. This sprint splits those same three things back into separate doors. That's a real change in direction, not an accident, and the room should vote on it knowing that.

## Look and feel
Same as the rest of the site: white, sparse, a lot of air, navy heading, left-aligned. Cards are flat panels with no shadow or border-heavy styling — plain rectangles of space, not designed tiles. It should read like a list of four short notes, not a dashboard.

## Left open
What "How we do things" actually contains, and what a card looks like with zero entries behind it long-term, were not decided this sprint.
