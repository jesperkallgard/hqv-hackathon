A customer standing in a shop wants to know three things in order: what is it, is it good, what does it cost. This page gives them exactly that and nothing else.

Top to bottom: a large clear photo, the model name and price right below it (big, bold, no digging), three plain-text specs that actually decide a purchase — weight, frame size range, warranty — and a full-width Buy Now button at the bottom.

Deliberately left out: reviews, testimonials, spec tables. That's trust-building for later, once we know people don't already believe the page without it.
