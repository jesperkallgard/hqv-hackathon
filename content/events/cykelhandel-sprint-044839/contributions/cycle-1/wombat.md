No nav, no footer, no reviews. Just what someone standing in front of the bike would want to know, in order: what it is, whether it's good, what it costs — then one line so they don't feel like they're gambling.

```block
type: BikeBuyPage
data:
  imageUrl: "https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1485965120184-e220f721d03e?w=1200&q=80"
  imageAlt: "The Harlow Road, side profile, plain background"
  modelName: "Harlow Road"
  price: "$1,895"
  specs:
    - label: "Frame size"
      value: "54 cm"
    - label: "Weight"
      value: "10.4 kg"
    - label: "Drivetrain"
      value: "Shimano 105, 2x11 speed"
    - label: "Wheels"
      value: "700c, tubeless-ready"
  buttonLabel: "Add to Cart"
  reassuranceLine: "Free returns within 30 days. 2-year frame warranty."
```

Single column, responsive, breathing room instead of clutter. The button doesn't wire up to a real cart yet — it just needs to look and feel like one you'd actually click.
