The start page shifts from a static front door to a more inviting entry point: three Citat or news highlights at the top, then a Kaj AI chat bar in the middle so people can ask what is for me and get help faster.

- Three Citat or news highlights sit on the page as the first thing people see, giving the page a hook instead of a long explanation.
- The page keeps a more visual, lighter feel by using highlight blocks rather than extra text-heavy intro content.
- Kaj becomes an AI chat bar in the middle of the start page, so the page can answer questions instead of only pointing to content.
- The start page is still for colleagues who need a quick reason to come here, not a wiki-style document or a meeting deck.
- The layout is a start page change only; it does not replace the separate show-and-tell view, tutorial view, or other dedicated pages.

Build a start page layout with three Citat or news highlights and a Kaj AI chat bar in the middle. The page has to answer the hook question: what is for me, and why should I come here? Keep it as a start page, not a document. The new content must be scan-first and visual, with the highlights carrying the first message and the chat bar offering help. Do not turn this into the monthly show and tell meeting view, the Sitecore tutorial, or a full redesign of the site.
