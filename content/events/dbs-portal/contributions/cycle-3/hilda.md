The site carries three content areas: Optimize, Explore, and Scale. Each one shows what the team is working on now and what is coming next, with a weekly cadence and a named owner.

- Optimize is for current work in the Optimize track, and David keeps it up to date.
- Explore is for current work in the Explore track, and Jesper keeps it up to date.
- Scale is for current work in the Scale track, and Madde keeps it up to date.
- Each area answers the same question in plain English: what are we working on now and what is in the pipeline.
- The page keeps only these three buckets for this sprint; the broader front-page families, metrics, release timelines, show and tell, and contacts remain out of scope here.

Build a web page, not a wiki: header with the site name and short navigation, one generous column of content, and a footer. Use the established white, airy layout with navy headings, left-aligned text, and fully round pill buttons. The page carries three content areas only: Optimize, Explore, and Scale. Each one must state what it is for, what is current, what is coming, and who owns it. Frequency is part of the content, so the weekly cadence must be visible. Use real content only; no placeholder text. Keep it simple enough to scan at a glance, and do not add the broader content families that were mentioned elsewhere.
