The site carries a named set of content areas, each with a clear purpose, a person who keeps it up to date, and a frequency so people know what is static and what changes.

- There is a list of ongoing activities with status so people can see what is in progress and what is current.
- There is a weekly update area with a low threshold for contribution so people actually add to it.
- There is a show and tell database for recently rolled out initiatives, with a short comment from Hilda, Olivia, or Niklas.
- There is a statistics area and a ticket status follower so people can check numbers and track follow-up work.
- There is a design wall where people can heart concepts and add ideas, so design thinking can collect feedback in one place.

Build a named list of the content the site carries, with what each kind is for, who keeps it up to date, and whether it is static, weekly, or monthly. Keep it as a web page, not a wiki: one card per area, readable at a glance, with a header, short navigation, one generous column, and a footer. Use real content only, with outsider-friendly wording and no DBS acronyms in headlines. Include images, graphs, sketches, and visual examples where they help, and keep the UI more alive and dynamic without losing the clean Husqvarna-style card feel. The design wall needs hearting and idea contribution. A logo is requested, but the exact site name and the final list of content labels are still not fully settled here.
