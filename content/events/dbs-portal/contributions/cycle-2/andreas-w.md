Open on the ideas section, then move into KPI cards and metrics. This start page puts the user’s contribution in front of the numbers, so the most useful thing is the easiest thing to reach.

- The ideas section is the biggest area on the page.
- KPI cards come second, directly under ideas.
- Metrics come after KPIs, as a smaller supporting section.
- The page still keeps contributions visible, but not bigger than ideas.
- Nothing else competes with the three main sections on the first screen.

## Start page order
- First and biggest: **Ideas**.
- Second: **KPI cards**.
- Third: **Metrics**.
- Keep **Contributions** visible, but not larger than ideas.
- Do not let any other section compete with the three main areas on the first screen.

## What each section carries
- **Ideas**: user-generated ideas and submitted requests.
- **KPI cards**: the headline numbers the page opens with.
- **Metrics**: the supporting data that explains the KPIs.
- **Contributions**: secondary supporting content, smaller than Ideas.

## Look and feel
- Keep the same internal website direction: white, airy, navy headings, left-aligned text.
- Use a real webpage layout with header, short navigation, one wide content column and footer.
- Buttons stay fully round pills: orange primary, navy outlined secondary.
- States stay as small round tags.
- Move closer to the Husqvarna brand guidelines: less is more, cleaner hierarchy, fewer competing elements.

## Content rules
- Use plain outsider names for sections.
- Keep copy short and direct.
- Real content only; no placeholder text.
- If a label already exists in the product, reuse it.
- Keep the start page focused on the one thing it is for: a fast overview of what matters most.

## Out of scope
- No extra dashboard modules.
- No new page flow.
- No separate deep-dive screens in this sprint.
