A lighter DBS start page for people who need the picture fast. It keeps the front door scan-first, adds clearer quick links, and shows release work as a timeline so teams can see what is next.

- The page opens with the main navigation, a short hero and the biggest outcome cards, not a long explanation.
- Quick links are expanded with knowledge base, show and tell documents, and direct links into initiative and topic pages.
- Show and tell content is split into smaller slices so people can jump straight to a specific initiative or topic.
- The release pipeline is shown as a timeline of expected releases, so the next items are visible at a glance.
- Metrics show the measurement date and the year-on-year comparison date, so every number is read in context.

- Keep the page as a start page, not a reading page.
- Remove the extra explanatory text that describes how the page works.
- Keep the top of the page focused on the page name, short navigation and the highest-value cards.
- Add quick links for the knowledge base, show and tell documents, and links into initiative/topic pages.
- Show and tell meetings must be sliceable into smaller parts by initiative or topic, so users can jump in without scanning the whole page.
- The release pipeline should be presented as a timeline of expected releases, not as a dense list.
- Add dates to every metric so it is clear when it was measured.
- Add year-on-year comparison dates alongside the metrics.
- Keep the content real and specific; do not replace it with placeholder copy.
- Keep the look aligned to the canon: web page, white, airy, navy headings, left-aligned text, pill buttons, small round state tags.
- No extra wiki-style prose at the top of the page.
- Keep the page designed for scanning, with main titles and read-more links rather than large blocks of text.
