The start page adds a real revenue post, written as a sales stat with the exact change and direction, so it can be surfaced without DBS context.

- Revenue is shown as one of the three things that make the start page a great start page.
- The revenue item is a post, not a label or a headline with no number.
- The post states the exact revenue claim: up, down, or flat, and by how much.
- The post is written for a colleague who does not work here, so it does not need DBS context to parse.
- If the revenue change is not known, the item stays open rather than being filled with placeholder content.

Build the revenue post as real content only. It has to say what changed and what it meant, not what was done. It needs the exact number or direction, because "Revenue" by itself is not enough. Keep it in the same start-page style: web page, one card per area, clear at a glance, white with lots of air, navy headings, left-aligned text, and a real internal website feel. Do not use internal acronyms in the headline. Do not add placeholder text. Frequency still needs to be stated, but it is open here whether this revenue item is static, monthly, or weekly until the actual content is known.
