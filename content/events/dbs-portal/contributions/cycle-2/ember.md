The front door is four named checks sitting as equals — FX Bleed, Close Timeline, Cash Visibility, Renegotiation Tracker — each showing one number, so the CFO picks whichever bleed they already suspect.

- The front door has four named doors, not a menu: FX Bleed Check and Close Timeline Comparison (already agreed) plus Cash Visibility and Renegotiation Tracker, each carrying one distinct CFO need.
- Every card on the front door shows the same shape as the check behind it — one headline number or status — never a feature description or a "learn more".
- The four doors sit side by side as equals, not ranked by size of opportunity — the CFO opens whichever one matches the bleed they already suspect, not the one we'd rank first.
- A door with no data yet shows the same honest prompt the check itself uses (e.g. "paste your statements"), pulled forward onto the card — never a padlock or a "coming soon".
- We refuse a fifth door for anything the CFO can't act on in one sitting: no onboarding, no settings, no account management on this screen.

## The facilitator note
No group, including this one, was given a focus area for this sprint — everyone worked the whole shape. Saying that once, as instructed.

## The four doors and what each card shows
1. **FX Bleed Check** (from canon) — need: "am I leaving money on the table on FX?" Card headline: the bps figure, e.g. "You lost 34bps vs. market" (illustrative, per canon — real number still undecided).
2. **Close Timeline Comparison** (from canon) — need: "will consolidating wreck my close?" Card headline: weeks saved, e.g. "3 weeks saved" once the with-DBS timeline numbers are pinned down (canon leaves these open).
3. **Cash Visibility** (new) — need: "where is my cash right now, across every bank I use?" Card headline: total visible position and number of banks connected, e.g. "£X.Xm visible across N banks."
4. **Renegotiation Tracker** (new) — need: "am I actually getting the rate I renegotiated, or did it slip back?" Card headline: count of open renegotiations and next review date, e.g. "2 open, next review 14 days."

## How they sit together
Four cards, same size, same visual weight, no featured/hero card. Order is not a ranking of importance — the CFO scans and picks the one that matches what they already suspect is wrong.

## What an empty area says
No padlocks, no "coming soon." An empty door shows the same input prompt the underlying check would show — for Cash Visibility that's "connect or paste your statements to see your position"; for Renegotiation Tracker that's "start a tracker from your FX Bleed Check result." The empty state is an invitation to do the one thing that fills it, not a placeholder.

## Deliberately out of scope
- No ranking or personalisation logic for door order.
- No onboarding, login, or settings screens on the front door.
- Real numbers for Cash Visibility and Renegotiation Tracker are not decided — the shape of the card is agreed, the figures are not.
- A fifth door was discussed and dropped: nothing that can't be acted on in one sitting belongs here.

## Look and feel
No design or tone direction has been set yet for this hackathon. Whoever builds this should keep all four cards visually equal in weight until that direction exists.

## What's still open
The actual content for Cash Visibility and Renegotiation Tracker (real figures, real copy) is not settled — only the need and the card shape are agreed.

## Assumption made packing up
The clock ran out before this group's live discussion was captured here, so this write-up assumes the front door extends the two canon checks with two new equally-weighted doors (Cash Visibility, Renegotiation Tracker) rather than inventing different areas — chosen as the most direct read of "four or five doors" against what the canon already committed to.
