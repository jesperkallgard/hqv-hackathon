The front door stays the sprint-one table. The five doors are a filter on it, named after who already owns rows there — Optimise, Scale, Explore, Platform, Support.

- The front door is the same one table from sprint one, not a new landing page — you land on it already, filtered to nothing, and pick a door from there.
- There are five doors, not three or four: Optimise, Scale, Explore, Platform, Support — named after owners already doing the work in the existing rows, not invented categories.
- A door is a filter on the one table, not a separate page — walking through Platform shows the same columns (Test, Status, Result, Owner, Updated), just fewer rows.
- Playbooks and Who to ask stay in the nav as they were; the five doors only replace the placeholder team list from sprint one, they don't add a fourth nav item.
- A door with no owned rows under it doesn't get built — this list is only as good as which of the five actually have owners claiming rows against it, and that wasn't checked this sprint.

## Written without a group conversation this sprint

The clock ran out before this group and I talked through the idea. This is the most defensible version built from the canon's own open question ("which named teams or areas exist for the team filter was not settled") and the direction, not something the group argued through. Treat every line below as a starting guess for the next sprint to correct, not a settled decision.

## The front door

No change from sprint one's page. You land on the single table — What changed, Playbooks, Who to ask in the nav, the grouped-by-status table (Rolled out / Running / Decided against) as the whole screen. No hero, no explainer, no dashboard chrome, per the existing canon and the direction.

## The five doors

Replace the three placeholder team names (Scale, Optimise, Explore) in the filter with five, matching the owners already sitting in canon's example rows so the doors are traceable to real teams rather than invented ones:

- **Optimise** — CRO team's tests (sticky nav, product comparison)
- **Scale** — Content team and Platform team's rollouts (delivery copy, payment method)
- **Explore** — Market operations' early rollouts (dealer stock)
- **Platform** — technical/infra changes that aren't a CRO test (currently folded into Scale's rows; this sprint pulls it out as its own door)
- **Support** — Support and web, Brand and web's calls, including decisions against (chat widget, video background)

A door is nothing more than the existing team filter, applied. It does not get its own page, its own heading style, or its own layout — it narrows the one table to the rows that owner group carries.

## What this doesn't settle

- Whether Platform and Support have enough of their own rows to justify being separate doors rather than folded back into Scale/Explore — not checked.
- Whether five is the right number long-term, or whether it grows as more teams start owning rows.
- The post detail page (what you get clicking a row) — still open from sprint one, still not this sprint's job.

## Look and feel

No change to the direction already set: white, sparse, navy headings, flat panels, no shadows, navy header row on the table, alternating light grey rows. The five doors show up as filter options, not as five separate coloured tiles or icons — nothing that needs a legend.
