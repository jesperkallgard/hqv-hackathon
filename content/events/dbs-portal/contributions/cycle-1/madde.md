A web page for Husqvarna employees to skim digital work at a glance. It opens on a dashboard of cards, then lets people expand into the detail they need.

- It is a web page, not a wiki, with one card per area and a dashboard first screen.
- It is called .comni, and employees open it.
- The first thing they do is skim the dashboard cards and expand any card they need.
- The five areas are completed initiatives, ongoing initiatives, SEO, Analytics, and Optimisation.
- It uses real content only, with short posts that say what changed and what it meant, not what was done.

# Sprint 1 hand-in

## What it is
A **real internal website** for Husqvarna employees, not a document or wiki. The page opens on a dashboard with cards that can be read at a glance and expanded for more detail.

## Name
**.comni**

## Who opens it
Employees.

## What they do first
They land on the dashboard and skim the cards for each area.

## Areas
There are **five** areas, named so an outsider can understand them:
1. Completed initiatives
2. Ongoing initiatives
3. SEO
4. Analytics
5. Optimisation

## Content rules
- Use **real content only**.
- Placeholder text counts as empty.
- Each post must say **what changed and what it meant**, not just what was done.
- Keep it short enough to scan.

## Look and feel
- White, airy, left-aligned.
- Navy headings.
- A generous single-column content area.
- Header with the site name and short navigation.
- Footer.
- Cards for the areas.
- Pills for buttons.
- Small round tags for states.

## Out of scope
- No wiki feel.
- No internal-acronym headlines.
- No extra area beyond the five above.
- No placeholder copy.
