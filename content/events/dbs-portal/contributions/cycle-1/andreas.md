A sales area for people who need to see whether the rollout is making money move. It shows the stat that matters, then says what changed and how it helped sell more.

- Puts the main sales number first, not buried in a report
- Shows which change moved the number and by how much
- Makes the link to selling more explicit
- Separates latest, ongoing, and coming sales releases
- Keeps the page readable at a glance as a real internal website

## What this area must do
- Show the sales statistic that best answers whether we are selling more.
- Explain the change behind the stat in plain English.
- Make the result understandable to someone outside the team.
- Keep the structure as one card per area in a web page, not a wiki.

## Content
- Use the real sales stat once it is known: revenue, conversion, orders, pipeline, or another agreed measure.
- If the measure is not decided yet, say that plainly instead of inventing one.
- Write what changed and what it meant; do not stop at activity.

## Look and feel
- White page, lots of air, navy headings, left-aligned text.
- Small round tags for states.
- Fully round pill buttons: orange primary, navy outline secondary.

## Out of scope
- No internal acronyms in the headline.
- No placeholder text.
- No broad strategy language without a number attached.
- No extra sections beyond the sales view and its explanation.
