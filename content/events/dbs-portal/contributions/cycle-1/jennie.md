A simple board for colleagues to see who is doing what, who owns each area, and who to contact when they need something. It is for people who need the right person fast, without digging through a wiki.

- It shows one board, not a document or a wiki.
- Each person card says what they do and how to reach them.
- Each area has one clear owner person, not a team.
- The board is read at a glance, with one card per area.
- It keeps the information current enough to be useful for contacting someone.

Build a web page, not a wiki. White, lots of air, navy headings, left-aligned text, with a header for the site name and a short navigation, one generous content column, and a footer.

The core idea is a planner board that shows who is doing what. It is for colleagues who need to find the right person and the right contact fast.

Content to show:
- A board of people and areas.
- For each person card: name, what they do, what area they own, and who to contact about that area.
- Use real content only. Do not use placeholder text.
- Treat owner as a person; do not use "the team" as an owner.
- Include the track and BU in the content where relevant, with one word each, because that avoids follow-up questions.
- Every post/card must state whether it is static, monthly, or weekly.

Feel:
- One card per area.
- Posts should be readable at a glance.
- Nothing that needs a guide beside it to parse.
- Use small round tags for states, not coloured words.
- Buttons are fully round pills: orange with white text for the primary action, outlined navy for the rest.

Copy rules:
- Write for a colleague who does not work here.
- No internal acronyms in headlines.
- Say what changed and what it meant, not what was done.

Out of scope:
- No wiki pages.
- No generic team owners.
- No placeholder blocks.
- No dense tables or anything that needs training to use.
