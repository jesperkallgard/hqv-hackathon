The page stops being a reading page and becomes a real internal website start page: one generous column, one card per area, and clear quick links for moving straight to the right content.

- The top explanatory text is removed from the page and moved elsewhere, so the home page stays short and does not need a guide beside it.
- More Quick links are added, including knowledge base, show and tell documents, and links to related initiatives or topics.
- Show and tell is split into smaller parts so people can jump straight to a specific initiative or topic instead of reading one long block.
- Metrics show when they were measured and the year-on-year comparison date, so the numbers carry their own context.
- The page keeps the feel of an official site: header with site name and short navigation, white space, navy headings, left-aligned text, pill buttons, and small round status tags.

Build the start page as a web page, not a wiki or document. It should be a jumping-off point, not a long explanation page, and the extra top-level explanation moves off the page. The content should be organised into clear cards so it can be taken in at a glance. Add the Quick links the room named, and let show and tell break into smaller slices for specific initiatives or topics. Make the metrics explicit about measurement date and year-on-year comparison. Keep the existing official-site look and feel: header, short navigation, one generous content column, footer, lots of air, white background, navy headings, left-aligned text, fully round pills, orange primary action, outlined navy secondary actions, and small round tags for states. Out of scope here are a new site, a full personalisable layout, and turning the page into a place for long-form storytelling or a planner board.
