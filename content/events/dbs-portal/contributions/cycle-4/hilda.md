The caller sends in the problem, CAI shows the responsible owner by name and team, and then offers three obvious next-step choices based on that problem.

- The handoff starts from a problem sent in by the caller, not from browsing or a manual search.
- CAI shows the responsible owner visibly, including both the owner’s name and team.
- CAI routes the problem straight to the owner immediately rather than after a follow-up question.
- After the handoff, CAI offers the next action instead of ending at the owner display.
- The next action is a small set of three choices, with the most obvious design choice chosen from the problem the caller gives it.

This is a CAI handoff change for the internal site. It has to take a problem from the caller, identify the responsible owner, show both the owner’s name and team, and send the problem on immediately. After that, it must offer the next action as a small set of three choices. The choice should be the most obvious design choice for the problem the person gives it. No extra follow-up question is kept in this version, and nothing beyond the owner handoff plus the three-choice next step is defined here.
