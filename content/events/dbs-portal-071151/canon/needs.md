# What Scale actually gets asked

This is a running list, not a status report. Each card is addressed to one kind of reader — the audience who would actually ask this — and answers them directly enough that nobody from Scale has to stand next to them and explain it.

```block
type: NeedCard
data:
  audience: "Market teams"
  tag: "husqvarna.com"
  headline: "My Husqvarna's login flow changed this month"
  whatItMeans: "The Dealer Locator also moved. If a customer mentions either, that's why — check what's live before you tell them something that's no longer true."
```

```block
type: NeedCard
data:
  audience: "Market teams"
  headline: "What test is running on their product family right now, and what it's measuring"
  whatItMeans: "Right now there's a new chainsaws landing page layout being tested, and we're tracking whether people scroll past the fold — not whether they buy. That's the number they should quote if someone asks how it's going."
```

```block
type: NeedCard
data:
  audience: "EU market teams"
  headline: "If the dealer experience changed, and what broke or got better"
  whatItMeans: "EU has tighter dealer relationships than US or PAC, so a surprise in the Dealer Locator lands on someone at a partner meeting, not just a support ticket."
```

```block
type: NeedCard
data:
  audience: "New joiners to DBS"
  headline: "What Scale actually owns versus the rest of Digital Solutions"
  whatItMeans: "Scale owns My Husqvarna, Dealer Locator, and Support pages on husqvarna.com. Explore owns the chatbot. Optimise runs tests across all of it. That's it — they stop guessing."
```

```block
type: NeedCard
data:
  audience: "IT"
  headline: "What's in the next Sitecore release from our team, and what we need from their side"
  whatItMeans: "Not a Gantt chart: we're pushing a checkout flow change in three weeks and need a UAT environment two weeks before that."
```

---

Scale is a team within Digital Solutions.

**The test:** if someone from Scale has to stand next to the reader and explain it, it doesn't belong here yet.

---

## Where this came from

This page carries forward the one card Quartz built in full, plus the four they wrote out in the same shape. Their idea: a need is written audience-first — who it's for, then what they need to know, then why it matters to them specifically — not as a general status update but addressed to one kind of reader at a time. All five needs and the shape they proposed carry through unchanged; nothing from the winning hand-in was cut.
