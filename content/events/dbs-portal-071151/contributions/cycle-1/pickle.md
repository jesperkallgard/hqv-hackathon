These are the needs Cleo hears every week in Slack, each tied to who's actually asking. Nothing invented — this is the list as it came in.

- **Market teams EU** — when Dealer Locator changes roll out in their region, and whether the new filtering will work for their dealer mix.
- **Market teams US** — what conversion lift we're seeing on the homepage test, so they can tell sales leadership whether digital is pulling weight.
- **Market teams PAC** — which product families we're optimizing for next quarter, so they know whether to brief retailers on new site features.
- **Brand** — what the test results mean for how we talk about husqvarna.com in campaigns — did sticky nav actually change how people browse.
- **IT** — what Sitecore patches are in the pipeline, and whether they'll break anything built in Kameleoon.
- **Management** — whether the investment in Scale, Optimise and Explore is moving revenue or just traffic.
- **New joiners in DBS** — what Scale actually owns versus what Support owns, and where the seams are.
- **Martin** — whether we're on track for the quarterly goals he set in January, without having to call Cleo.

That's eight named audiences, nine needs (Market teams split three ways by region). Each one is a real question with a real person or team behind it — not a guess at what they might want to know.

**Left open:** we wanted input from whoever owns Explore and whoever manages the test roadmap — they'd likely add more without trying. We ran out of time to pull them in this sprint, so this list is Group Pickle's view only, not the full picture. We also built a card-per-need layout to match the front-door look, but it didn't finish compiling before the clock ran out, so this is handed in as a plain list instead. The words are real; the shape is the thing to fix next.
