The facilitator hadn't split this sprint into parts when we started, so we picked the area we thought was ours: turning the needs list into a small number of doors an outsider would recognize on sight, rather than a wiki they have to search.

We landed on four:

- **What's changing on the site** — the one people check the moment they spot something different, or before they tell a customer something that might no longer be true.
- **How we're doing** — the health of the three tracks in one place, so nobody has to chase us for it.
- **What's coming** — the pipeline, written so market teams can plan, IT can resource, and brand can brief ahead of it.
- **Who we are and how to reach us** — what DBS owns, who owns what inside the team, and how to get hold of us.

Every need in the current list fits under one of these without forcing it, and each name is something you'd recognize without anyone from Scale standing next to you.

**Still open:** we split five existing needs plus a few we know exist but haven't written up yet (like the Sitecore release one) across these four cards from memory, not from a full audit of every need in the room. Someone needs to walk the whole list against these four doors and check nothing falls between them before this becomes the actual front page.
