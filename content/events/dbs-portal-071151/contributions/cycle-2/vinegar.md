The needs list isn't a status report to be filed under headings — it's five things real people asked, and each one has to live somewhere an outsider would look first. We named four areas an outsider would recognise, and mapped every existing need to one of them. One area has nothing in it yet, and we're saying so rather than inventing content to fill it.

**Coming changes** — what's shipping to husqvarna.com and when. Holds the My Husqvarna login flow change, the Dealer Locator move, and the Sitecore release note to IT.

**Test results** — what's being tested and what it means for the brand, not the internal metric. Holds the chainsaws landing page test.

**How we work** — the shape of the team, for anyone who needs to stop guessing what Scale owns. Holds the "what Scale owns vs the rest of Digital Solutions" card.

**Who to ask** — the person who owns each track, each BU, each thing someone outside DBS needs to unblock. Nothing in the needs list backs this yet — no need has been written as "who owns this." We're naming the door without inventing who's behind it. That's this room's gap to fill next, not ours to guess at.

One assumption, made because the clock ran out and nobody was there to confirm it: we left "Who to ask" in the grid as an open door rather than dropping it, because naming a real gap is worth more than pretending it isn't a need — but we did not invent names or owners to fill it. Also flagging once, as agreed: the facilitator hadn't split this sprint into parts when we started, so this is our claim on the whole naming question, not a slice of it.
