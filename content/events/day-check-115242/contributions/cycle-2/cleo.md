One column, four flat panels, the four names from the direction and no others — an outsider lands here and knows where to click before they've read a word.

- The page is four cards in one column and nothing else — no nav bar, no search box, no featured item, no "latest updates" strip repeating what the cards already say.
- The four cards are named exactly "What's changed", "What we're testing", "How we do things", "Who owns what" — not renamed, not shortened, not turned into friendlier marketing labels.
- The order is fixed, not alphabetical and not most-recent-first: What's changed, What we're testing, How we do things, Who owns what, in that order every time.
- Every card carries the same three things and no more: the name, one line saying what's behind it, and the date that area last changed — the same one-line discipline the feed already uses.
- All four cards are the same size and weight — flat panels, no colour, no shadow, no card bigger than another — because the page is a directory, not a pitch for one of the four.

## What it is
The homepage. One column, white background, a lot of air, navy headings — nothing else on the page above or below the four cards.

## The four cards, in this order and with this exact text
1. **What's changed** — "What shipped and what's being tried, one line at a time." Last updated: 12 March (the latest date in the What's changed feed today).
2. **What we're testing** — "The experiments running right now, and when they end." No page exists behind this yet — the What's changed feed already carries test rows (see canon), so this card may end up pointing at a filtered view of that same feed rather than a new page. That decision is open, not made here.
3. **How we do things** — "The way the team works, written down once instead of explained again." No page exists behind this yet.
4. **Who owns what** — "Who to ask, for what, without guessing." No page exists behind this yet.

## Look and feel
Same as the feed: white, sparse, one column, navy headings, flat panels with no shadow, text left-aligned. Each card is a plain rectangle with a thin rule or hairline border, not a coloured block — it should look like a list of four things someone wrote, not four buttons someone designed.

## What it refuses to do
No icons on the cards. No card described in more than one line. No card visually bigger or brighter than another, even if one page behind it has more content than the others. No fifth card, ever — the direction says four areas, no more, and this page is where that rule is enforced.

## Left open
Only "What's changed" has a real page behind it today (from sprint 1). The other three cards point at areas that don't exist yet — what "What we're testing" shows once built, and whether it's its own page or a filtered version of the existing feed, is not decided here and is the next sprint's question, not this one's.
