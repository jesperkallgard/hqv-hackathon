One screen, one list: what changed on the site, what's being tested, and who owns what. Newest first, nothing else.

- One list, one screen, newest first — no search box, no filters, no tabs.
- The feed answers exactly three kinds of question: what changed, what's being tested, who owns what. Nothing that isn't one of those three gets posted.
- Every row is one line, plus an owner's name and a date. No detail page to click into for the answer.
- Ownership facts aren't pinned above the feed as a separate section — they're rows like any other, re-posted on a cadence so they stay near the top instead of going stale and getting buried.
- If a row wouldn't stop someone asking a colleague, it doesn't belong in the list — no announcements, no marketing copy, no 'we're excited to'.

## What it is
The whole first screen is one feed, titled **What's changed**. Newest first. No search box, no filters, no tabs, no pagination beyond scroll.

## What a row looks like
One line of plain text, plus an owner's name, plus a date. That's it — three things, always in that order. No thumbnails, no tags, no "read more".

Example rows, to show the three kinds of thing that belong here:

- Dealer locator search rebuilt to use postcode instead of town name. — Bo, 12 March
- Dealer locator ownership confirmed — still Bo. — Bo, 3 March
- A/B test running on the pricing page: old layout vs new. Ends Friday. — Dag, 10 March
- Homepage hero copy changed to match new tagline. — Dag, 8 March

## How ownership stays current, without a pinned section
"Who owns the dealer locator" doesn't change often, but it still has to answer the question weeks after it was last true. There is no separate "ownership" box sitting above the feed — that would break the "one list, nothing else" rule. Instead, an owner re-posts a short confirmation row on a set cadence (e.g. monthly) even when nothing changed: "Dealer locator ownership confirmed — still Bo." That row's only job is to bump the fact back near the top of the same feed everything else lives in.

## What every row must have
- An owner's name (a person, not a team).
- A date.
- How often that thing gets checked or updated — either stated in the line itself ("Ends Friday", "confirmed monthly") or understood from the type of row (a change is a one-off; an ownership row recurs).

## Look and feel
White background, a lot of air between rows. Navy heading ("What's changed"), left-aligned, one column. Flat panels, no shadows, no card borders trying to look designed — closer to a page someone typed than a page someone built.

## Out of scope
- No search, no filtering, no categorisation UI.
- No comments or reactions on a row.
- No detail pages — if the one line and the owner and the date aren't enough to answer the question, the row is wrong, not the format.
